LAT 5 BUSINESS PERSONAL PROPERTY SELF-REPORTING FORM ONLINE FILING INSTRUCTIONS

 

NOTE:

To expedite the filing of your LAT5 online, before starting the form, it is advisable to gather the information about your business personal property first, and write the details on the printed form.   The system will log you out if there is no activity for 30 minutes.

 

Login Screen:

Only businesses listed on our tax rolls in the prior tax year will be able to file LAT5 form online.  All business owners should receive a letter which will include the Tax bill Number and PIN that you will use to login.

Before you can enter the tax bill number and PIN provided in the letter, you must first create an online account. Click the “Create Account” button on the login page and fill in the required fields.

NOTE: IF YOU ARE NOT THE BUSINESS OWNER, YOU MUST SUBMIT A NOTARIZED AUTHORIZATION FORM AND EMAIL IT TO personalproperty@orleansassessors.com

Once you’ve created an account, log in

·         File a new LAT 5

·         See the status of your LAT 5 filing(s)

LAT 5 Section 1 – Inventories / Merchandise and Cost of Goods Used

All inventories/merchandise for sale and cost of goods used must be entered under this section.  YOU SHOULD REPORT THE REPLACEMENT VALUE OF YOUR INVENTORY IN ITS EXISTING CONDITION, NOT THE AMOUNT USED OR SOLD DURING EACH MONTH.  You do not need to make an entry in each column, only those that apply to your business. Your taxes will be determiend only by the amount reported, not the column you enter it in.  $1,000 worth of merchandise will be taxed the same as $1,000 worth of raw materials or work in progress, etc. 

Enter the monthly average of each category for each  month inventory is taken.  Once a number is entered hit the Enter/Return key on your keyboard to save.

Inventory values will be averaged by this office. Click  when complete.

LAT 5 Section 2 – Furniture and Fixtures

All furniture and fixtures owned by the business must be entered in this section.  Please group all your furniture and fixtures by year of acquisition.    All fully depreciated assets must also be reported.

Select the property type from the dropdown menu by clicking on a cell in the Property Type column. Enter acquisition year and cost in appropriate columns, hitting the Enter/Return  key on your keyboard to save after each.

You may delete a row by clicking on the respective cell under Property Type and selecting Delete Row.

Click  when complete.

LAT 5 Section 3 – Machinery and Equipment

All machinery and Equipment owned by your business must be entered in this section.  This includes computer software, computer hardware, office equipment, etc.

Please group all different types of assets by year of acquisition to minimize data entry.  All fully depreciated assets must also be reported.

Directions are the same as Section 2.

LAT 5 Section 4 – Leasehold Improvements and Miscellaneous Property

Leasehold improvements include building renovations made by the lessor of a building.  Any personal property which cannot be classified under section 1-3, should be entered in this section.

Please group all assets by year of acquisition to minimize data entry.  All fully depreciated assets must also be reported.

 

Directions are the same as Sections 2 and 3 except in this section you must also provide the item name/description in the Item column.

LAT 5 Section 5 – Consigned Goods, Leased, Loaned or Rented Equipment, Furniture, Etc.

All business properties in your possession that you do not own must be reported under this section.  This includes consigned goods, rented machinery and equipment, and furniture and fixtures.

Note:  Inventories that you  own must be entered under Section 1 of this form.

Below are the descriptions of each field:

·         Property description – description of the consigned property, rented equipment, etc.

·         Owner name – Provide the name of the owner of the consigned, loaned, and rented personal property.

·         Owner Address – Provide the complete mailing address of the owner of the consigned, loaned, and rented personal property.

·         Phone Number – Provide the contact phone number of the owner of the consigned, loaned, and rented personal property.

·         Age of Property - Provide the age of the loaned or rented personal property.

·         Monthly Rental - Provide the monthly rental of the rented personal property.

There are no drop down boxes on this page. Please fill in all columns for all applicable assets.

Click  when complete.

LAT 5 – Validation

Please review your rendition before submitting. If everything is correct we recommend printing out a copy or saving it as a pdf using the print button in the top left corner.

If changes need to be made, click “Previous” to go back to the page with an error.

Click  when complete.

LAT 5 Signature and Verification

After you have reviewed the form, the next step is to verify and sign the form.

Signature and verification would require you to enter the following information:

·         Taxpayer Name –  Tax payer or business name will be displayed.

·         Tax preparer Name – Enter the person preparing your LAT5.  This can be your accountant, clerk etc.  If you are the owner and prepare you own LAT5, you can also enter the owner name here.

·         Tax Preparer Phone No. – We require the tax preparer phone number if we need to contact you about your LAT5.

·         Tax Preparer email -   We send you an acknowledgement that we received your LAT5 using this email address.  This serves as a confirmation that we received the LAT5 that you filed online.  You will be required to enter you email twice to verify that you entered your correct email address.

·         PIN -  Enter the PIN provided in your LAT 5 mailing to sign the on-line form. 

You can click on the  button to submit your LAT5 form.   After you submit your form, you will not be allowed to make corrections.     The home screen will change the status to ‘Submitted’ after you submit the form.

Click  button to go back to the validation screen.
